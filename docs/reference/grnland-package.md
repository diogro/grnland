# grnland: Evolution of Gene Regulatory Networks on Adaptive Landscapes

Individual-based simulations of populations whose phenotypes are the
steady states of gene regulatory networks, evolving on arbitrary
adaptive landscapes. Networks have any number of genes and arbitrary
activating or repressing interactions (Hill-type kinetics), or are given
as user-defined ODE systems. Provides analytical sensitivities and
mutational (M) matrices of the genotype-phenotype map.
